This site is where I publish [writing](/writing/) and keep a [contact page](/contact/). `danlopez.fyi` also hosts a **personal AI agent** — a self-hosted assistant that I run on a private server for my own use. It is not a public product: it has exactly one user, me.

## What it does

The agent takes care of everyday digital chores on my own accounts:

- reads, summarizes, labels, filters, drafts and archives my email
- keeps my calendar in order and prepares daily briefs
- reads and writes documents, spreadsheets and files I keep in my own drive
- answers questions by searching my own mail, calendar and documents

## What it accesses

With my permission, granted through Google's OAuth 2.0 consent flow, the agent can access my Gmail (read, modify, send), Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Google Contacts. Access exists only for the account I personally authorize, and I can revoke it at any time from my Google Account.
